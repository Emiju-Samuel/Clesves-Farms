Our Story
Clesves Farms began with a passion for nature, sustainability, and food security. Over the years, we’ve grown into a trusted agricultural brand in Nigeria, producing high-quality cocoa, cassava, and garri, while offering professional consultation services to upcoming farmers. At Clesves, we believe farming is more than just cultivation, it’s a commitment to nourishing people, empowering communities, and protecting the environment

Farm Setup & Planning
